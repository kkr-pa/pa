Actionable Intelligence for Better Learning & Teaching.

We're more than a quarter-century into the 21st century and still ...

Our assessment platform is the fix.

Students write on paper as usual.
Teachers assign scores onscreen.

Minimal technology required

Teachers need access to a computer, a printer and a scanner.
Students need access (post-test only) to a computer, tablet or cellphone.

For teachers

Assign scores question by question (shown in video), quickly, consistently. Get question-wise performance analytics (shown in video), automated scores totals and error-free transfer to supported electronic gradebooks.

For students

See their performance alongside their peers (shown in video) and learn from classmates' answers.

For parents

Parents and their student can read, discuss, and learn from anonymous model answers selected by the teacher from classmates' responses.

Who it's for

In our experience, students do better when teachers resist the urge to jump in the moment a student meets difficulty. Teachers make room for curiosity, conversation, struggle, even frustration and intervene only if sustained effort still leaves unanswered questions. This way, students have the opportunity to grow into independent, self-regulated learners.


Not every school will agree with this approach.
Our platform is built for those that do.
